Meet the 7 agents

Each role has a plain-English job. LangGraph runs the floor; Risk can reject before a memo is written.

01Market Scout

Finds and ranks opportunities across a universe.

02Technical Analyst

Reads RSI, MACD, moving averages, trend, and bias.

03Fundamental Analyst

Checks financials, valuation, and business quality.

04News Analyst

Summarizes headlines, catalysts, and sentiment.

05Quant Analyst

Scores edge, win probability, and scenarios.

06Risk Manager

Stress-tests downside and sizing — can REJECT.

07Portfolio Manager

Writes the final investment memo and paper allocation.

      FAQ — Dinesh AI Fund
      
      
      What it is, who built it, and how demo differs from live trading.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        What is Dinesh AI Fund?
        
      An open 7-agent investment research floor. You input a ticker; agents scout, analyze charts/financials/news, score edge, stress-test risk, and ship a paper investment memo. Built for education and experimentation — not a black-box trading bot.
      
      
      
      
      
        Who founded Dinesh AI Fund?
        
      Dineshgopi Sunkara founded Dinesh AI Fund. Source and issues live on GitHub.
      
      
      
      
      
        Is the sample / demo live trading?
        
      No. Demo mode uses bundled or simulated data. The product does not handle real money, does not execute live trades, and is not financial advice. Live mode only enriches analysis with an optional Anthropic API key — still paper research.
      
      
      
      
      
        What do the 7 agents actually do?
        
      01 Market Scout ranks opportunities · 02 Technical Analyst reads charts · 03 Fundamental Analyst checks financials · 04 News Analyst weighs headlines · 05 Quant Analyst scores edge · 06 Risk Manager can REJECT · 07 Portfolio Manager writes the final memo.
      
      
      
      
      
        What sample examples can I browse?
        
      Example A — TSLA: full 7-agent walkthrough ending in HOLD/WATCH. Example B — NVDA: abbreviated gallery with a BUY memo. Example C — RIVN: Risk Manager REJECTS to show capital protection. All bundled demo data.
